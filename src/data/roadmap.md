# **CompX 2026**

## **Building a Layered Financial System**

CompX is entering a new phase.

2026 is not about adding more features. It is about turning CompX into a **cohesive financial system** \- one that is easier to understand, safer to use, and able to scale without accumulating hidden risk.

This document outlines **why** we are making changes, **what** we are building, and **how** the CompX ecosystem evolves through 2026\.

---

## **Why We're Doing This**

Across DeFi, a familiar pattern has emerged:

* Protocols grow by shipping features  
* Features accumulate into complexity  
* Complexity increases risk, cost, and user confusion  
* Progress eventually slows under its own weight

CompX reached a natural inflection point.

By the end of 2025, the ecosystem had powerful primitives \- lending, stablecoins, incentives, payments, governance \- but those pieces were increasingly interconnected in ways that made future growth harder, not easier.

The 2026 strategy exists to **reverse that trajectory**.

Instead of building wider, CompX is building **deeper**.

---

## **The Core Idea: Layers**

Financial systems scale through **structure**, not features.

The most resilient systems:

* Separate authority from execution  
* Separate incentives from strategy  
* Separate primitives from products

In 2026, CompX formalises itself as a **layered stack**:

### **Layer 1 \- Primitives & Infrastructure**

Defines truth, execution, and risk boundaries.

### **Layer 2 \- Composition & Strategy**

Combines primitives into intent-driven experiences.

### **Layer 3 \- Financial Instruments**

Packages strategies into ownable, legible products.

Each higher layer may only *compose* the layer beneath it.  
Lower layers never depend on higher ones.

This keeps complexity **contained instead of cumulative**.

---

## **The CompX Stack (2026)**

### **Core (Layer 1\)**

The authoritative control plane of the ecosystem.

* COMPX governance & FLUX  
* xUSD stablecoin (xUSD v2)  
* Canonical protocol state  
* Identity and engagement via X-NFT

In 2026, the main CompX app becomes **Core**:

**app.compx.io → core.compx.io**

Core is focused, conservative, and long-term by design.

---

### **Orbital (Layer 1\)**

Capital efficiency and lending.

Orbital provides:

* Lending and borrowing markets  
* Broad asset support  
* Collateral acquisition via loan buyouts

Orbital expands what is *possible* with capital \- it does not optimise outcomes.

---

### **NovaDEX (Layer 1\)**

Liquidity, execution, and settlement.

NovaDEX is CompX's native automated market maker:

* Constant-product pools  
* LP-first economics  
* Predictable execution

NovaDEX acts as the execution backbone for swaps, routing, and integrations across the ecosystem.

---

### **Delta (Layer 1\)**

The neutral incentives network.

Delta is where staking and farming live:

* Permissionless pool creation  
* Long-running incentive programs  
* Any ASA supported

Incentives are no longer embedded inside Core.  
They live where they belong \- in a neutral, dedicated layer.

---

### **Waypoint (Layer 1\)**

Programmable money.

Waypoint enables:

* Invoices and payment routes  
* Stablecoin-native payments  
* Real-world settlement endpoints

Waypoint is designed as infrastructure, not a SaaS product.

---

### **Canix (Layer 1\)**

Discovery and decision-making.

Canix surfaces opportunities across Algorand:

* Neutral comparisons  
* Wallet-aware insights  
* Sponsored boosts (xUSD)

Canix controls **attention**, not capital.

---

### **The Drop (Layer 1\)**

NFT marketplace and distribution primitive.

The Drop is CompX's native NFT layer, designed around **controlled scarcity and time-based distribution** rather than infinite listings.

Key characteristics:

* Full-featured NFT marketplace (fixed price, auctions)  
* Proprietary **reverse-auction launch mechanism**  
* Unsold NFTs are burned at auction end, permanently reducing supply

This creates:

* Anti-hoarding dynamics  
* Clear price discovery over time  
* NFTs that behave more like ephemeral drops than permanent inventory

The Drop uses **xUSD** as a primary pricing and settlement asset, with governance (FLUX) controlling launch parameters and platform rules.

---

## **Strategy & Composition (Layer 2\)**

Layer 2 products turn primitives into outcomes without introducing new financial risk.

### **Turbine**

A strategy flywheel that lets users:

* Deposit once  
* Access lending, liquidity, and incentives  
* Rebalance within clearly defined bounds

All strategy changes are governed by participants.

---

### **Haven**

A consumer-facing xUSD savings experience.

Haven is designed to:

* Abstract DeFi complexity entirely  
* Present xUSD as a simple, trustworthy savings product  
* Act as an onboarding funnel for non-crypto-native users

Haven does not optimise yield. It prioritises **clarity, safety, and simplicity**.

---

### **Assembly**

Governance-as-a-Service.

Assembly externalises CompX governance infrastructure so that:

* DAOs and communities can run votes and proposals  
* Governance logic is reusable and auditable  
* CompX governance primitives become ecosystem infrastructure

Assembly is not a social platform or token launcher.

---

### **Cairn**

Transparency and reporting layer.

Cairn provides:

* Treasury visibility  
* Protocol and DAO reporting  
* Real-time observability for on-chain systems

Cairn increases trust by making CompX and third-party systems legible to users, governance participants, and partners.

---

### **Launchpad / Composer**

A canonical surface for projects to:

* Create tokens  
* Bootstrap liquidity  
* Launch incentives  
* Surface opportunities

It assembles primitives \- it does not price or arbitrate.

---

## **Economic Alignment**

### **xUSD**

xUSD is the economic centre of gravity across CompX.

Every product either:

* Uses xUSD directly, or  
* Strengthens xUSD liquidity, demand, or duration

---

### **FLUX**

FLUX represents long-term alignment with CompX.

It is used to:

* Govern parameters  
* Approve strategies  
* Improve experience

FLUX is not a paywall.
